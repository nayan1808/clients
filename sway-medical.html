Portfolio/Sway Medical

Holding 03 · Healthcare SaaS · to acquisition, Oct 2025

The channel call that took deal-to-close from 16% to 49%

At a $500 to $1,000 average deal, one bad lead worked by sales erases the margin on a good one. The question wasn't how to get more leads; it was why the existing ones weren't closing.

01 · Situation

Small deals, no room for waste

Sway Medical sells concussion and balance testing software to athletic trainers and clinics. Google Ads was producing volume, but only 16% of created deals closed, and at this deal size the unit economics die the moment sales works the wrong lead.

02 · Hypothesis

Search intent can't separate this buyer from lookalikes, but profession-based targeting can

The diagnosis pointed to a traffic quality ceiling, not a campaign defect: the search terms available simply couldn't distinguish an athletic trainer with budget from students, patients, and researchers typing the same words. If that held, moving budget to Meta, where the audience is reachable by profession and interest, should raise close rates even at similar volumes. And for a considered clinical product, a demo ask on first touch was probably too much; a webinar in the middle should convert skeptics that direct response couldn't.

03 · Method

Reallocate, insert a middle step, filter the funnel

  • Channel reallocation: moved budget from Google to Meta Ads; Meta delivered direct response plus a brand lift that showed up as growth in branded search demand.
  • Webinar funnel: launched as the bridge between cold attention and a sales conversation for skeptical clinicians.
  • ICP anchoring: refined audience targeting and anchored ads and nurture emails in the ICP's actual pain points.
  • Lifecycle filters: built HubSpot filters that kept non-ICP leads out of the sales funnel entirely, so sales only touched leads worth their time.
  • LinkedIn layer: added LinkedIn Ads for the clinic-side buying committee.
Finding At a $750 average deal, the win isn't more leads. It's never letting sales touch the wrong ones.

04 · Result

Close rate tripled, funnel proved out

Deal-created-to-closed-won went from 16% to 49% (Q1 2026 vs Q2/Q3/Q4 2026). The webinar funnel drove $51K in closed deals on $8K of spend at $6–7 per MQL. Roughly $200K in closed revenue overall, with the LinkedIn layer influencing another $135K in pipeline. The company carried that trajectory into its acquisition by Healthy Roster in October 2025.
