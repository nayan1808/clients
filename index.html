Operating capabilities

What I actually do

Google Ads

Search · Demand Gen · YouTube

  • Keywords split into brand defense, competitor, category, and problem intent, each with its own campaign, budget, and CPA target
  • Weekly search term reviews: junk goes to negatives, converting queries get their own ad groups
  • RSA copy written in the ICP's own search language, then tested against live query reports
  • Offline conversions imported from the CRM so bidding learns from qualified leads, not raw form fills
  • Demand Gen, YouTube, and a Microsoft Ads mirror once search volume hits its ceiling

LinkedIn Ads & ABM

Account-based, committee-aware

  • Named account lists tiered 1:1, 1:few, and 1:many, with a play and budget per tier
  • Cold ads run point-of-view content; only retargeting asks for the demo
  • Retargeting expanded across the buying committee, not just the form-filler
  • Audience size floors and frequency caps checked so small TAMs don't burn out
  • Judged on engaged pipeline in the CRM, not clicks

Meta Ads

B2B, B2C & D2C eCommerce

  • Contact-based B2B targeting at a fraction of LinkedIn CPMs
  • One creative angle per segment, new tests launched weekly
  • Hook rate, hold rate, and placement breakdowns read before any creative verdict
  • Acquisition, catalog, and retention split into separate campaigns with exclusions between them
  • Rolling reach checked for audience saturation before budgets scale

Landing Pages & CRO

Message match · testing

  • The page continues the sentence the ad started: same promise, same language
  • One page per persona and intent level, not one page for everyone
  • Copy anchored in objections pulled from sales calls and reviews, answered in the order they come up
  • Hypothesis-first A/B tests, one variable at a time, sample size checked before launch
  • Wins measured on qualified conversions, not raw conversion rate

Measurement & Attribution

GA4 · GTM · HubSpot · Salesforce

  • GA4 and GTM audited before budgets move: events, deduplication, consent
  • Lead status and closed revenue synced back from HubSpot and Salesforce
  • Platform, CRM, and self-reported attribution read together, each trusted for what it can prove
  • Pipeline reviews lag-adjusted so young campaigns aren't killed before the sales cycle matures
  • Reporting starts at pipeline and revenue; MQL counts stay off the first slide

Demand Gen Strategy

Budgets · funnels · GTM

  • Budgets worked backwards from the revenue target through each funnel conversion rate
  • Spend split between demand capture and demand creation, sized by category search volume
  • Intermediate offers between the ad and the demo so cold traffic has a next step
  • Email nurture mapped from first touch through post-demo follow-up
  • Custom Claude Code agents for research, copy, and landing page production

Method

Same sequence, every engagement
STEP 1

Diagnose before spending

Audit the funnel, the tracking, and the lead quality history before touching budgets. At Pest Share, the 2024 problem wasn't traffic, it was who the ads were talking to.

STEP 2

Anchor everything in the ICP

Ads and landing pages built around the buyer's actual pain points and objections, pulled from sales calls and research, not from a brainstorm doc.

STEP 3

Build the full funnel

Paid search and social, ABM where the deal size earns it, landing pages tested with CRO specialists, and nurture emails from first touch through post-demo.

STEP 4

Report in revenue

Pipeline and closed-won in HubSpot, Salesforce, and Fibbler, presented to founders and CMOs weekly. MQL counts don't appear on the first slide.
