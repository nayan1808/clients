01 · Situation

Six-figure contracts, weather-driven demand

National Facility Contractors sells commercial facility services where a single contract can be worth six or seven figures, and the moment of need is dictated by weather, compliance deadlines, and fiscal calendars. Demand for parking lot repairs and demand for snow removal don't peak in the same month.

02 · Hypothesis

In seasonal categories, budget pacing is the strategy

One evergreen campaign structure would average away the account's edge: overspending in dead months and starving the weeks when facilities managers actually buy. If each service line got its own campaign, budget curve, and landing page matched to its own season, the same total spend should produce meaningfully more pipeline. The rest is execution.

03 · Method

A system built on the service calendar

  • Service-specific campaigns for each line of business, scheduled and budgeted around its own seasonal demand curve.
  • Landing pages built to convert per service, so a facilities manager searching for one specific problem landed on that problem, not a generic services page.
  • Full-funnel coverage from urgent, high-intent searches to the earlier research queries that fill next quarter's pipeline.
Finding Paid search here doesn't need to close the deal. It needs to reliably put the company in the room, season after season.

04 · Result

A pipeline machine that runs on the calendar

Over a year and a half, the program contributed to roughly $14M in open pipeline, with each service line's campaigns earning their budget in their own season rather than competing for one shared pool.
