Holding 01 · B2B SaaS · PropTech · 2024–2026

The inbound rebuild behind a 3x ARR year

In 2024, only 13 of every 100 marketing leads booked a demo. Before changing anything, the question was whether the campaigns were broken or aimed at the wrong buyer. The answer decided everything that followed.

01 · Situation

Healthy lead volume, terrible CRM

Pest Share sells pest-control coverage software into property management companies. Coming out of 2024, lead volume looked fine on paper and terrible in HubSpot: an MQL-to-demo rate of 13% meant sales spent most of its time disqualifying. The engagement brief was blunt: fix lead quality, then grow.

02 · Hypothesis

The campaigns aren't broken. The targeting is aimed at the wrong buyer.

The first pass through the account showed campaigns that were mechanically sound: sensible structure, reasonable costs, no tracking gaps. So the old strategy wasn't wholesale wrong, and tearing it down blind would have thrown away working machinery. The evidence pointed somewhere specific: the ads and pages spoke to a generic property manager, not to the person who actually feels the pest problem and buys software to fix it. If that was true, fixing the ICP and message would move lead quality without touching budget levels.

03 · Method

Rebuild the GTM from the ICP up

  • Research first: who at a property management company feels the pest problem, what words they use for it, and which objections kill deals. Every ad and landing page was rewritten around those pain points before a dollar of new spend went out.
  • LinkedIn Ads (ABM): account-based campaigns against a defined target list, with account-level attribution wired through Fibbler and pipeline truth in HubSpot.
  • Google Search: intent capture rebuilt on the same messaging, so the click and the page told one story.
  • Landing pages: weekly creative testing with CRO-focused pages that held 4–6% conversion rates through scaling.
  • Nurture: partnered on full-funnel email, first touch through post-demo, so leads that weren't ready yet didn't leak out of the funnel.
Finding The 2024 problem wasn't traffic. It was who the ads were talking to.

04 · Result

Hypothesis confirmed, then compounded

Lead quality moved first: MQL-to-demo climbed from 13% to 55% and held through Q1 2026. Deal value followed, up 230% year over year in MRR terms, with closed revenue up 205%. The LinkedIn ABM layer engaged target accounts representing $4.92M in open pipeline on $21K of spend across seven months.

ARR grew from $410K to $1.23M, roughly 23% above the growth target committed at engagement start. Marketing and sales alignment around the same funnel contributed to the company's $28M Series A in September 2025.
