The short version

Companies usually call me after the easy explanations have failed. The agency was replaced, the budget was raised, the ads were refreshed, and pipeline still didn't move. Almost every time, the diagnosis is the same: the ads, the landing page, and the sales conversation are each describing a different buyer.

Pest Share is the cleanest example. They came in with a 13% MQL-to-demo rate and everyone blamed the campaigns. The campaigns were fine. The targeting was built for a buyer who doesn't sign contracts. We rebuilt the strategy around the person who actually feels the pest problem, and the same budget took MQL-to-demo to 55% and tripled ARR in a year.

That's the job as I see it: find out what's actually true about the buyer, then make every dollar of spend agree with it.
